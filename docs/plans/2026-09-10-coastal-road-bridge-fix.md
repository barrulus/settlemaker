# Gormaca: scalloped coast and an unnecessary bridge

Reported against production v2.4.0. The exact decoded FMG envelope is retained
in [`tests/fixtures/gormaca-coast.json`](../../tests/fixtures/gormaca-coast.json):
population 10, seed 4080411970, two measured royal approaches and a trail.
It supplies `oceanBearing`, but no river or other water polygons. The coastline
is therefore generated by settlemaker's bearing-only fallback.

## Causes and changes

The fallback scaled both shoreline distance and bay wavelengths by the square
root of population. At ten people its principal wavelength was only about
87 metres, although the drawn landscape spans roughly 275 × 340 metres.
Repeated small scallops filled the tile. Bay wavelengths now have a landscape
minimum (360 metres for the principal wave). Distance from the village and
amplitude still follow population, so this does not push a tiny hamlet's sea
hundreds of metres away. Explicit input polygons are unchanged.

The coastal road offset its samples onto dry land, but did not check the
segments connecting them. One chord cut across a small inlet for 9.78 metres;
exact crossing detection then correctly recorded that overlap and the renderer
painted a timber bridge. The missing decision was in road placement: a road
following a shore should stay on its bank. Each proposed offset now checks its
entire connecting segment against the water polygons before accepting it,
trying the existing bounded inland offsets if needed. The genuine short-river
crossing path remains supported.

Gormaca now has no wet road intervals, no bridges, full housing and all three
contract entry points preserved. Its coastal road reaches the frame without a
stopped-road diagnostic. This is a geometry fix, not suppression of a bridge
symbol over an unchanged wet road.

## Review and validation

`output/coast-fix/index.html` compares the exact payload against v2.4.0. Both
sides use identical seed, frame and drawing scale; baseline source uses its
original coastline and road placement. The generated SVGs and models sit beside
that page.

New regressions cover the captured FMG payload, a concave coast whose dry
samples used to join through water, and the number of shoreline turns visible
around the tiny settlement. The artificial coast test also requires the road
to escape, so deleting/stopping the road cannot pass it.

Both 56-village review panels retain full housing and zero building-access
failures. The city controls remain byte-identical. Brook seeds 3 and 103 retain
seven real crossings each (longest spans 6.48 and 6.97 metres).

Focused coast/apron/bridge/tile-edge checks passed 52 tests, and the three new
regressions passed. TypeScript compilation, script type checks and browser build
passed. The full suite passed all 1,299 tests in 125 files with two workers
in 237 seconds, with a clean exit and no unhandled errors.

No URL fields or output schemas change. This branch does not update the
production web pin or replace the published v2.4.0 tag.
